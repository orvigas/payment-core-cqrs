# ADR-NNN: Short decision title

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-NNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Deciders: who was involved

## Context

What situation forced a decision. Constraints, requirements, and the options that were realistically on the table. Assume the reader has no memory of the original discussion.

## Decision

The choice that was made, stated plainly in one or two sentences.

## Rationale

Why this option won over the alternatives. Name the rejected alternatives and the reason each lost; that is the part future readers need most.

## Consequences

- What becomes easier.
- What becomes harder or riskier, and how that risk is handled.
- Follow-up work the decision creates.

## References

Links to related ADRs, knowledge entries (`[[name]]`), or external documentation.
